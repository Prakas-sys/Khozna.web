Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 Coverage: eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS & Bank Transfers

1. Khozna Escrow Protection

All booking fees and room deposits paid through the Khozna App are automatically held in Khozna Escrow. Deposit funds remain locked in escrow until the Seeker inspects the property or approves move-in.

2. Seeker Cancellation Terms

  • Free Cancellation (100% Refund): Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled property visit qualify for a 100% full refund to the original payment source.
  • Late Cancellation (within 24 hours): Cancellations submitted within 24 hours of the scheduled visit incur a nominal processing fee (NPR 100 or 5%), with the remaining balance refunded immediately.

3. Host Default & Non-Availability

If a Host cancels a scheduled visit, fails to show up, or informs the Seeker that the room is no longer available:

🛡️ 100% Instant Refund Guarantee

The Seeker receives a 100% full refund of all fees, and penalty points are assigned against the Host's account.

4. Video & Photo Misrepresentation Guarantee

If the physical room during inspection differs drastically from the video walkthrough or photos on Khozna (e.g. broken structure, missing promised amenities, wrong floor):

  1. Select "Report Misrepresentation" in the Khozna App within 2 hours of the visit.
  2. Upon support team verification, a 100% full refund is issued immediately.

5. Refund Processing Timelines (SLA)

  • Digital Wallets (eSewa / Khalti): Credited within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Bank Account Transfers: Credited within 3 to 5 business days per Nepal interbank settlement rules.

Host & Property Owner Agreement

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 Target: Property Owners, Landlords & Authorized Agents in Nepal

1. Host Eligibility & Authorization

To list a room or property on Khozna, you warrant that you are the legal property owner or hold valid written authorization (subletting permissions) from the owner.

2. Listing Quality & Video Standards

  • Real Video Walkthroughs: Videos and photos must accurately portray the room's current state. Stock photos or deceptive media are strictly prohibited.
  • Geographic Precision: The property location pinned on Khozna must match the exact building location.
  • Functional Amenities: Amenities listed (Water, WiFi, Parking, Balcony) must be fully available to tenants as advertised.

3. Transparent Rent & No Hidden Surcharges

The monthly rent displayed on Khozna must be the complete rental cost. Hosts may NOT demand unannounced extra fees or broker commissions upon tenant arrival.

4. Escrow Payouts & Commission

Booking deposits are secured in Khozna Escrow. Upon tenant visit completion or move-in approval, funds are transferred directly to your eSewa, Khalti, or Bank account minus Khozna’s nominal platform service fee.

5. Host Cancellation Penalties

Canceling accepted visit requests without a verified emergency degrades user experience. Defaulting hosts will suffer search ranking penalties and risk permanent account suspension.
