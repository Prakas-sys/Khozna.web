We are currently in private testing. The public launch in Kathmandu Valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur) is happening very soon. Everyone on the waitlist gets priority access!

Is Khozna free to use for renters? ›

Yes! It is 100% free for renters to search, chat with owners, and visit properties without paying any broker fees or commissions.
